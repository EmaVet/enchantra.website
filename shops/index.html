Where Decks Find You

A small woo woo directory of shops worth the trip.

Some people choose a deck. Some walk into a shop and a deck chooses them. These are shops across Australia and Aotearoa New Zealand where you can hold the cards before you take them home, picked for their decks and for how it feels to walk in.

No shop pays to be here, and we earn nothing from your visit. Many of these shops also offer readings. We list them for their decks and their welcome, so ask the shop about anything else.

Hours change, especially in smaller towns. Call ahead if you're driving a long way.
