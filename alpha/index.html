Keepers · the alpha team

You are one of the first few people to hold Enchantra.

Thank you for doing this. We call our testers Keepers. Enchantra has been made quietly, one moon at a time, and now it is time for others to help shape it. What you notice first will shape what it becomes.

You don't need to know tarot, and you don't need to have tested an app before. This page tells you everything, in order.

What Enchantra is

A daily practice, kept in lunar cycles.

You pull real cards from your own tarot deck. Enchantra reads with you, in the way your particular deck is meant to be read, and remembers. It reflects; it never predicts.

The app keeps time by the moon. A lunar cycle runs from one New Moon to the next, about a month, with the Full Moon halfway through.

Every day
One card. A short reading, and a deeper one if you want it.
New Moon
Three cards to open the cycle, and an intention, something you want to hold in mind, for the month ahead.
Full Moon
A three card check-in, to see how the intention is going.
Before the next New Moon
Enchantra gathers your readings, your journal and your echoes, and gives you a reflection on the cycle you are closing, before you set the next intention. We call that reflection the cycle close.

Two places that are yours

Journal. What a card meant to you, what came up when you drew it, anything at all. You can come back to any entry and add to it; nothing here is ever written by Enchantra. This is your space.

Echoes. Those small coincidences that seem to follow you around: the same number everywhere, a bird that keeps appearing, a song, a dream, a symbol you keep meeting. Some people call them synchronicities; we call them echoes. Note them as they happen, and Enchantra will notice when one returns.

The app has five tabs along the bottom: Moon home, Journal, Patterns, Echoes and You. You is where your account and settings live.

What alpha means

You get to move through the app like nobody else will.

An alpha is the first, unfinished version of an app, given to a few people to try before anyone else. Enchantra follows the real moon, so once it is released, everyone meets it one day at a time: a card today, the Full Moon in a fortnight, the close of the cycle a fortnight after that. You don't have to wait. Your copy of the app has a small tester button at the top right of every screen, and behind it, three powers.

Load a season
Drop months of history into your app in one tap, as if you had been using it since April, so you can see what Enchantra does once it knows you.
Move the clock
+1 day and +7 days move the whole app forward. Draw several days' cards in one sitting, reach the Full Moon this afternoon, close a cycle tonight.
Go anywhere
Jump straight to any part of the app, whatever the moon says.

These powers exist only in the alpha. When the beta begins they go, and everyone after you will meet Enchantra at the moon's own pace. So use them, and enjoy them; you are seeing the whole of the app in a way nobody else will.

What is in it for you

Enchantra is yours, for good.

When the app goes live, you move with it: first into the beta, the next and larger round of testers, then into the app as everyone else will know it. You will never pay for Enchantra. Not a trial, not a discount, but the whole of it, free, for as long as it exists.

All we ask in return is that you keep telling us what you think and how it feels, for as long as you love using it. The day you stop loving it, we want to hear that most of all.

What we hope to learn from you

Five questions. Keep them in the back of your mind.

Do you understand what Enchantra is for, without being told?
Is it easy to use? Where do you hesitate, tap the wrong thing, or get lost?
How does it look and feel on your phone? Anything cramped, cut off, or hard to read?
Do you need more instruction, or is it intuitive enough on its own?
Does it feel like a wise companion who remembers your journey, or like a fortune teller?

The last one matters most. Enchantra is meant to hand the meaning back to you. If a reading ever felt like it was telling your future, or telling you off, we want to know exactly where.

Getting in

A deck, the app, and your account.

A deck of your own

You will need a tarot deck. Any deck will do, and it does not need to be new or special. If you don't have one, tell M before you start and she will help you find one. You don't need to know what the cards mean; Enchantra reads them with you.

On Android

M will send you a link ending in .apk. Tap it on your phone, let it download, open the downloaded file and tap Install. Your phone will say the app comes from an unknown source and ask you to allow installs from your browser; allow it, that is normal for an app that isn't on the Play Store yet. If Play Protect says it doesn't recognise the app, let it scan, then choose to install anyway.

On iPhone

M will invite you by email to TestFlight, Apple's own app for trying apps before release. Install TestFlight from the App Store first, then open the invitation email on your phone and tap the button in it. TestFlight shows Enchantra with an Install button. Updates come through TestFlight too.

Your account

Open Enchantra, tap New here? Create an account, and enter your email and a password of at least eight characters. The app shows Check your email; within a minute or two an email arrives with the subject Confirm your email address. It comes from a plain technical address rather than from Enchantra and may land in spam. Tap the link, a page from the Enchantra website opens, and that means it worked. Back in the app, tap Back to log in and log in.

This is a real account. What you write is encrypted on your phone, and a copy is kept under your account on Enchantra's server so it survives a new phone. Nobody reads it. You can delete the account and everything in it from the You tab whenever you like.

Before you do anything else

Nobody has seen Enchantra on a phone like yours, and phones draw text differently. Take a screenshot of the first three screens you see (creating the account, the welcome screen, and Which deck do you draw with?), then one of the home screen with the moon once you reach it, and email the four to keeper@enchantratarot.com. Then answer the setup questions as yourself, and read the page called Before you begin. It is the promise the app makes to you.

To take a screenshot on an iPhone, press the side button and the volume up button at the same time. On Android, press the power button and the volume down button at the same time. The picture lands in your photos.

Your first run

Twenty minutes to begin, the rest whenever you like.

The first three steps take about twenty minutes and show you most of what Enchantra is; longer if tarot is new to you, and that is fine. There is no wrong way to do this. The other three steps can wait for another evening, or for the moon itself. Everything below starts from the tester button at the top right. The tester screen has three parts: the clock, load a season, and go to.

  1. Under load a season, load Five cycles, Tarot de Marseille. Five months of one person's practice appear, drawn with a deck called the Tarot de Marseille. You don't need to own that deck; the app switches to it so the history makes sense.
  2. Look at Moon home. Then Patterns: tap a day on the ring, the circle of days, and tap again today on an echo. Then Your moons, and open the cycle close of a finished cycle, the reflection Enchantra wrote at its end. This is what Enchantra becomes once it knows you.
  3. Under go to, tap Daily draw. Draw a real card from your own deck and read it. At the bottom of the reading there is a box to ask Enchantra a question about the card; try it. That is a good place to stop for the first sitting.
  4. Full Moon draw: make the check-in with three real cards.
  5. New Moon: close and begin: watch the cycle close (Enchantra writes this one, so give it a moment), then set an intention for the next.
  6. Load One cycle, Rider-Waite-Smith (another deck, the most common one) and do the same, to see it with a thinner history: this is what someone sees in their first month.

Two things to know. Loading a season replaces whatever is in the app, and any cards you draw afterwards join that season's story. And the clock lives only while the app is open; close it fully and it returns to real time, so just load the season again.

Then use it as yourself

This is the most useful thing you can do. On the You tab, tap Start over. It wipes the seasons and everything drawn during them and signs you out. Log back in, answer the setup questions again, and you begin clean: one real card a day from your own deck, for as many days as you like, without the tester button.

Telling us what you found

Two ways, and both are easy.

Every reading ends with the question Did this land? and two answers, Yes and Not quite. Either is useful. Not quite lets you say what was off in a couple of taps. These are sent without your name attached: we see what you said, not who said it.

For everything else, send it to M: email keeper@enchantratarot.com, or message M directly if you have her number. Screenshot freely. Take one of anything that strikes you, good or odd, and send it with a line about what you tapped just before; a screenshot is worth more to us than a paragraph, and you can never send too many. After your first run, three sentences alongside them is plenty: what confused you, what delighted you, what you would change. Worth a screenshot:

  • anything that looks wrong, cut off or hard to read
  • anything that made you wait more than a few seconds
  • a reading that felt like prediction, or like a verdict
  • a word or phrase that didn't sound like the app
  • anything that made you smile

If a screen ever looks empty, load a season first; a few screens need a history behind them. If the app ever closes on its own, a crash report reaches us automatically with your writing stripped out, but a note saying what you were doing still helps.

the small print

Nothing on the tester screen exists in the real app. Your own readings in the alpha are real, saved to your account, and yours to delete from the You tab at any time. Enchantra reflects, it never predicts, and it is not a substitute for care from a person. If anything on this page is unclear, tell M; that is useful feedback too.
