Tarot card shops in Australia and New Zealand

Where Decks Find You

A small woo woo directory of shops worth the trip.

Some people choose a deck. Some walk into a shop and a deck chooses them. These are shops across Australia and Aotearoa New Zealand where you can hold the cards before you take them home.

No shop pays to be here. Call ahead if you're driving a long way.

Australia

New South Wales

Tarot card shops in Jervis Bay, Sydney and Penrith.

Three shops

Kiki The Hippie Shop

Huskisson, Jervis Bay

A colourful shop between the post office and the bakery, with tarot and oracle cards, crystals, handmade jewellery and upcycled clothing.

  • Address: 5/62 Owen St, Huskisson NSW
  • Open: Call ahead for hours

Crow and Cauldron

Peakhurst, Sydney

Violet's witch shop in Sydney's south, stocking tarot cards and spell supplies. She reads and teaches there too.

  • Address: 50 Baumans Rd, Peakhurst NSW
  • Open: Call ahead for hours

The Cauldron of Wellbeing

Penrith

Lynne's witchcraft and Pagan shop at the back of Parker Arcade, with a large range of tarot and oracle cards. Free parking across the road.

  • Address: Shop 7–9, Parker Arcade, 476 High St, Penrith NSW
  • Open: Monday to Saturday

Australia

Victoria

Tarot card shops in Ballarat, Daylesford and Melbourne.

Four shops

Phoenix Alternative Body & Soul

Ballarat

A crystal shop in the Bridge Mall with tarot cards and its own oils, and an upstairs studio for sound baths and Reiki classes.

  • Address: 73 Bridge Mall, Ballarat VIC
  • Open: Monday to Saturday

The Empress & Wolf

Daylesford

Elicia's shop began as a gallery. Tarot, oracle and Lenormand decks downstairs, and readers in the rooms upstairs.

  • Address: 35 Vincent St, Daylesford VIC
  • Open: Call ahead for hours

SpellBox

Melbourne CBD

Danae Moon's witch shop in the Royal Arcade, with tarot cards and books, readings and classes on witchcraft. Its sister shop, The SpellBox Cauldron, is a minute's walk away in Block Place.

  • Address: 17 Royal Arcade, Melbourne VIC
  • Open: Every day

Temple of the Divine

Windsor, Melbourne

Jocelyne turned the hair salon where she trained as a teenager into a witch shop. Ask about the unopened vintage tarot decks.

  • Address: 48 Chapel St, Windsor VIC
  • Open: Tuesday to Saturday

Queensland

Tarot card shops in Cairns, Gold Coast and Brisbane.

Three shops

Crystal Ball Bookstore

Cairns

An independent Cairns bookstore since 1988, a few steps from Rusty's Markets, with a tarot reader in store every day.

  • Address: 91 Grafton St, Cairns QLD
  • Open: Every day

Gypsy Moon

Labrador, Gold Coast

Kim's family-run shop carries more than 180 tarot decks and 240 oracle decks, beside her own dreamcatchers and mandalas.

  • Address: 29C Musgrave Ave, Labrador QLD
  • Open: Call ahead for hours

Tarotopia

Strathpine, Brisbane

Rebecca's shop is all about cards, with more than 2,500 tarot and oracle products. She reads in store too.

  • Address: Shop 3, 4 Lincoln St, Strathpine QLD
  • Open: Tuesday to Saturday, and the second and fourth Sunday

Tarot card shops in Nelson and Whanganui.

Nelson

When people ask

Buying tarot cards in person

Where can I buy tarot cards in person in Australia?

In specialist tarot, witch and crystal shops, many of which let you look at sample decks before you buy. This page lists 16 across New South Wales, Victoria, Queensland, Western Australia, South Australia, the ACT and the Northern Territory, each checked against the shop's own website.

Where can I buy tarot cards in Melbourne?

SpellBox (Melbourne CBD) and Temple of the Divine (Windsor). Each listing has the address, opening days and a link to the shop.

Crow and Cauldron (Peakhurst) and The Cauldron of Wellbeing (Penrith). Each listing has the address, opening days and a link to the shop.

Tarotopia (Strathpine). Each listing has the address, opening days and a link to the shop.

Eternal Spirit (Unley). Each listing has the address, opening days and a link to the shop.

The Crystal Chalice (Braddon). Each listing has the address, opening days and a link to the shop.

Where can I buy tarot cards in New Zealand?

Mystic Journey (Nelson) and The Woo-Woo Shop (Whanganui). Check each listing for opening days; some visits are by appointment.

Should I buy a tarot deck in a shop or online?

In a shop you can look through sample decks and feel the card stock before you choose. If you can't get there, 11 of these shops also sell online: look for the bag button on their card.

Do shops pay to be listed here?

No shop pays to be here, and we earn nothing from your visit.

How do I suggest a tarot shop?

Email us at keeper@enchantratarot.com with the shop's name and town. We check every shop before it goes on the list.
