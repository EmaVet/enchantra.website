What Enchantra is

Enchantra supports a reflective practice with your own physical tarot deck. You draw the cards; the app helps you explore what they might mean in the context of your life, remembers your practice across lunar cycles, and shows you patterns over time.

Readings are generated by an AI language model. They are offered as material to reflect with, and the meaning you take from them is yours to make.
