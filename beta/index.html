Keepers · join the beta

Become an Enchantra Keeper.

You draw the cards. You tell me the truth. Enchantra becomes what you need her to be.

Enchantra has been built over months, and is ready now for real decks, in real hands, across real lunar cycles.

Keepers are the people who test Enchantra and shape what comes next. You bring the deck you already love, live with the practice, and tell me what breaks and what sings. Every message reaches me directly, and changes what Enchantra becomes. And in return, Enchantra is always free, as long as you keep reading and sharing what you notice.

What is in it for you

Everything is included, always.

Every reading, every tradition, everything included from the first day. When Enchantra opens to everyone and the subscription begins, your account stays free.

You draw from your own deck, just as you always would. Thoth, Rider–Waite–Smith and Marseille are each complete at 78 cards, and decks that grow from those traditions are welcome too. Set an intention at the new moon, return to your cards when you need to, and write down what they stir in you. Over months, patterns emerge.

New features reach the Keepers first. What you notice becomes what I build.

What a Keeper does

A Keeper keeps a regular practice, and shares the truth.

A keeper loves tarot, or is tarot curious. You don't have to know tarot to join. You have to enjoy a regular practice, daily if possible, and keep me informed about what you love, what you like, and what you are unsure of.

You give this feedback in the app. Every so often I might send you a question about a new interface, or a new feature to test before it goes public, or ask how new brand and marketing feels.

You are never locked in. If you need to step away when life asks, that is okay, and you are always welcome to return. Enchantra stays free as long as you are happy to give me feedback and share honestly what will make Enchantra better for you, and ultimately for others.

What we hope to learn from you

You don't need to be an expert, just curious.

Three kinds of noticing. An honest reaction is worth more than a careful one.

The readings
Did the reading land? Did it sound like a guide who knows the cards? Tell me where it rang true, and where it drifted.
The experience
Where did you pause? What did you expect that did not arrive? Anything that made you stop and wonder is worth telling me.
The care
Write to me while you are reading. A question, a problem, anything at all. Then tell me how it felt to be answered.

What Enchantra is

We love the moon, she keeps our time.

At the new moon
You set an intention for the cycle, then shuffle and draw an opening spread from your own deck.
Through the month
A single card on the days you reach for one. The practice waits for you, and you can step away and return whenever you like.
At the full moon
A closing spread, and a look back over the cycle you have just walked.
As the cycle seals
The month is gathered into something you keep. Then the next one begins.

A full cycle runs about twenty nine days. You can join at any point in the month, and all I ask is that you stay for one whole cycle, new moon to new moon, so you meet the practice complete. Enchantra grows richer the longer she remembers you, so keep reading after that for as long as the practice serves you.

What you need

Bring your deck, your phone, and curiosity.

  • A physical tarot deck you already own and like to hold
  • An iPhone or an Android phone
  • To be 16 or over
  • One full cycle, new moon to new moon, at whatever pace suits you, and the willingness to keep answering afterwards

The small print

The promises I am making you.

What it costs
Nothing. Enchantra stays free for as long as you are a Keeper.
Your practice
Your practice stays with you and carries into the full release.
Your privacy
What you write is encrypted on your phone and held for you alone. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
Changes
Readings will shift as I tune the engine. If anything ever needs resetting, I will tell you first.
Coming and going
Whenever you like. Life gets busy and seasons change. Write to me when you want to pick your practice back up.

The Keeper terms set all of this out properly, including what happens to the ideas you send me and what stays yours.

If a reading ever lands badly, this is what happens.

Enchantra is a reflective practice that sits alongside therapy, medicine and crisis care.

If a reading ever lands badly, tell me. There is a way to flag the reading in the app, and the flag comes straight to me. In the app, Feeling unsteady? sits at the foot of every screen and opens support lines checked for your region, any time you need them. In an emergency, call your local emergency number. 000 in Australia, 111 in New Zealand.

You draw the cards. Enchantra keeps the thread.
